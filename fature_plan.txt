## 1. Project Overview

NityaSadhana is a devotional practice tracking system designed to help users record and monitor their daily spiritual activities.

The system includes:
- User registration and login
- Dashboard
- Daily Sadhana Report
- Japa tracking
- Book Reading tracking
- Hearing tracking
- Seva tracking
- Weekly and monthly statistics
- Report History
- My Progress
- My Profile
